Personal injury in Hollywood

High tourist traffic, heavy rideshare density, and a freeway interchange that never slows down. Hollywood is built for accidents.

Hollywood sees a collision profile unlike most of Los Angeles. The US-101 Hollywood Freeway — one of the most congested urban freeways in the country — splits through the heart of the neighborhood. Hollywood Boulevard and Sunset Boulevard carry a constant mix of commuters, tourists, rideshare vehicles, and pedestrians that produces consistent pedestrian and vehicle conflicts at nearly every major intersection.

The density of entertainment venues, hotels, bars, and nightclubs along the Sunset Strip and in the Walk of Fame district also creates a distinct category of premises liability claims — inadequate security, overcrowding injuries, and property hazards in commercial spaces that see thousands of visitors daily.

Law Dog handles every type of personal injury case arising in Hollywood. We work on pure contingency — no fee unless we win, no upfront cost, no risk to you. We come to you anywhere in Hollywood, the Hollywood Hills, East Hollywood, Thai Town, or Little Armenia. The consultation is free, 24/7.

California's two-year statute under CCP § 335.1 applies to most Hollywood injury claims. If the City of Los Angeles, LADOT, or Metro is involved — a pothole on Hollywood Blvd, a Metro bus collision, an LAPD vehicle — you have just six months under Gov. Code § 911.2. Call us today.

Hollywood's specific injury landscape

Why Hollywood cases
are different.

Hollywood's unique mix of tourist density, entertainment venues, and freeway infrastructure creates injury patterns we know how to build cases around.

US-101 Hollywood Freeway
One of the most congested urban freeways in the country. The four-level interchange at the 101/110 merge is a consistent high-crash zone. High-speed collisions here often result in severe injuries with multiple liable parties.
Hollywood & Highland Intersection
The intersection of Hollywood Blvd and Highland Ave handles massive pedestrian and vehicle volumes around the Dolby Theatre, Ovation Hollywood, and the Walk of Fame. Pedestrian strike rates here are among the highest in the neighborhood.
Sunset Strip Venues
Bars, nightclubs, and music venues along the Sunset Strip generate premises liability claims involving inadequate security, overcrowding, poorly lit parking structures, and alcohol-related incidents. Property owners have a duty of care to every guest.
Rideshare Pickup Congestion
Hollywood's volume of Uber and Lyft pickup activity — particularly around the Walk of Fame, Pantages Theatre, and Hollywood Bowl — creates high-risk conditions for pedestrians stepping into vehicle lanes and for cyclists navigating rideshare-blocked bike lanes.
Hollywood Hills Road Conditions
Narrow canyon roads through the Hollywood Hills — Mulholland Drive, Laurel Canyon Blvd, Beachwood Canyon — feature sharp turns, poor lighting, and limited sight lines. Crashes here often involve disputed fault and require accident reconstruction.
Tourist Distraction Factor
Out-of-state and international tourists unfamiliar with California traffic laws and local driving patterns contribute to collision rates on Hollywood Blvd and surrounding streets. If a tourist driver caused your injury, their insurance still covers California accidents — we pursue every available policy.
