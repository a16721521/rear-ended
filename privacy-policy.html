Privacy Policy

Effective June 1, 2026 · Last updated June 2026

Who We Are

Law Dog Legal Group, APC ("Law Dog," "we," "us," or "our") operates the website at getlawdog.com. We are a California personal injury law firm. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you visit our website or submit an inquiry through our intake form.

If you have questions about this policy, contact us at hello@getlawdog.com or call (833) 4LAWDOG.

Information We Collect

Information you provide directly. When you submit our case intake form ("Get Started"), we collect:

  • First and last name
  • Phone number
  • Email address (optional)
  • Case type (e.g., car accident, slip and fall)
  • Approximate date of incident
  • Whether you have received medical care
  • Current symptom status

Information collected automatically. When you visit our website, we and our service providers automatically collect certain technical information, including:

  • IP address and general geographic location
  • Browser type, operating system, and device type
  • Pages visited, time spent, and referring URL
  • Click and interaction data

This information is collected through cookies and similar technologies, including Google Analytics 4 (described below).

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to your inquiry and evaluate whether we can assist with your legal matter
  • Contact you by phone or email regarding your submission
  • Analyze website traffic and improve our content and user experience
  • Comply with legal obligations

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for their marketing purposes.

Cookies and Google Analytics

Our website uses Google Analytics 4 (GA4), a web analytics service provided by Google LLC. GA4 collects data about your website activity through cookies and sends it to Google servers. We use this data to understand how visitors use our site and to improve our content.

Google Analytics may set cookies in your browser. You can opt out of Google Analytics tracking by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on. You can also control cookies through your browser settings.

For more information on how Google uses data collected through our site, visit Google's Privacy Policy.

Third-Party Service Providers

We use the following service providers who may process your information on our behalf:

  • Cloudflare, Inc. — Website hosting, CDN, and security services. Cloudflare may process request metadata (including IP addresses) to deliver our website and protect against abuse.
  • Google LLC — Analytics (Google Analytics 4).

These providers are contractually obligated to protect your information and may only use it to perform services for us.

Data Retention

We retain intake form submissions for as long as reasonably necessary to evaluate your inquiry and for legitimate business and legal purposes. Analytics data is retained per Google's default retention settings.

To request deletion of your personal information, contact us at hello@getlawdog.com.

California Privacy Rights (CCPA / CPRA)

If you are a California resident, the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA) provide you with the following rights regarding your personal information:

  • Right to know. You may request that we disclose the categories and specific pieces of personal information we have collected about you, as well as how it is used and shared.
  • Right to delete. You may request that we delete personal information we have collected, subject to certain exceptions.
  • Right to correct. You may request that we correct inaccurate personal information we hold about you.
  • Right to opt out of sale or sharing. We do not sell or share your personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.
  • Right to non-discrimination. We will not discriminate against you for exercising your privacy rights.

To exercise these rights, contact us at hello@getlawdog.com or call (833) 4LAWDOG. We will respond within 45 days of a verifiable request.

No Attorney-Client Relationship

Submitting information through our website does not create an attorney-client relationship. No attorney-client relationship is formed until both parties have signed a written fee agreement. Information submitted before that agreement is not protected by attorney-client privilege solely by virtue of submission through this site.

Children's Privacy

Our website is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such information, contact us and we will delete it promptly.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. Continued use of our website after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
