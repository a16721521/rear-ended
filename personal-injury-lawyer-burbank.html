Personal injury in Burbank

An entertainment industry hub with an airport, a major freeway interchange, and its own city government.

Burbank is its own incorporated city — separate from Los Angeles — home to Warner Bros., Disney, and Nickelodeon, and to Hollywood Burbank Airport. That combination produces a distinct set of injury patterns: predictable rush-hour surges tied to studio call times, airport rideshare congestion at pickup and dropoff curbs, and one of the busiest freeway junctions in the San Fernando Valley where the I-5 and SR-134 meet.

Downtown Burbank's shopping and dining corridor along San Fernando Boulevard draws heavy foot traffic, creating its own category of premises liability claims. And because Burbank is a separate municipality from Los Angeles, government entity claims here work differently — a government tort claim goes against the City of Burbank, not LADOT or the City of Los Angeles, and the airport itself is operated by a separate joint powers authority.

Law Dog handles every type of personal injury case arising in Burbank. We work on pure contingency — no fee unless we win, no upfront cost, no risk to you. We come to you anywhere in Burbank, Magnolia Park, or the Media District. The consultation is free, 24/7.

California's two-year statute under CCP § 335.1 applies to most Burbank injury claims. If the City of Burbank is involved — a road defect, a City vehicle — you have just six months under Gov. Code § 911.2. Call us today.

Burbank's airport, studios, and freeway interchange create injury patterns — and legal nuances — we know how to build cases around.

I-5 & SR-134 Interchange
One of the busiest freeway junctions in the San Fernando Valley, with frequent high-speed collisions, particularly during studio shift-change hours when traffic surges predictably each weekday.
Hollywood Burbank Airport Curbside
Designated rideshare pickup and dropoff zones see constant vehicle movement, double-parking, and pedestrians crossing between terminal and curbside areas — a recurring source of both vehicle and pedestrian claims.
Studio Commute Traffic
Warner Bros., Disney, and other studio lots generate predictable rush-hour surges tied to crew call times and production schedules, concentrated on Alameda Ave, Olive Ave, and Victory Blvd near the Media District.
Downtown Burbank & San Fernando Blvd
Burbank's shopping and dining corridor draws heavy foot traffic to retail and restaurants, creating a distinct category of premises liability claims involving parking structures and pedestrian walkways.
Separate Municipal Government
Burbank is its own incorporated city — government tort claims here go against the City of Burbank, not LADOT or the City of Los Angeles. Getting the right defendant and deadline correct from day one is essential.
Chandler Bikeway
Burbank's rail-to-trail bike path draws cyclists across the city, with vehicle crossings at street intersections creating cyclist-vehicle conflict points that require the same three-foot passing analysis as anywhere else in the county.
